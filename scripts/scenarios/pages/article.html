The last lighthouse on Sample Point

The lighthouse on Sample Point has stood above the harbour since 1881. Its lamp was converted to electricity in 1932 and automated in 1989.

For most of its life it was run by a single family, who kept the log books that the local museum now displays.

The restoration

In 2024 a volunteer group raised the money to repaint the tower and replace the rusted gallery railings.

The work took fourteen months, mostly because the weather on the point allows painting on only a few days each month.

Visiting

The tower opens to visitors on the first Sunday of each month.

The current keeper's cat, a grey tabby named Biscuit, greets every visitor at the door.
